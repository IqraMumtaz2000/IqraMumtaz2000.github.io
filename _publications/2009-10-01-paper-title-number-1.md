## Overview

This systematic review examines secondary immunodeficiency (SID) across multiple acquired clinical conditions and proposes a cross-domain pathway framework for understanding immune dysfunction and risk stratification.

## Research focus

The review investigates how different SID-producing conditions converge across three major immune pathways:

- **Humoral dysfunction:** impaired antibody production and functional antibody responses.
- **Cellular dysfunction:** disruption of T-cell and other cellular immune functions.
- **Innate immune dysfunction:** alterations in innate immune responses and inflammatory regulation.

## Methodology

The study followed PRISMA 2020 guidelines and used a structured qualitative evidence synthesis. Searches across MEDLINE, Embase, Scopus, and Web of Science identified 11,968 records. Following screening and eligibility assessment, 100 studies were included in the qualitative synthesis.

## Main contribution

The study proposes an exploratory, cross-domain SID Risk Stratification Framework integrating immunological findings, infection history, therapeutic exposures, and host-related risk factors.

The framework aims to provide a common conceptual approach for researchers and clinicians examining secondary immunodeficiency across different specialties and healthcare settings.

**Research note:** The proposed framework is exploratory and has not undergone prospective clinical validation. It should not be interpreted as a validated clinical prediction tool or used independently to guide patient management.
