My research focuses on the intersection of health economics, immunology, healthcare innovation, and digital transformation. I am particularly interested in understanding how evidence, data analytics, and emerging technologies can improve healthcare decision-making, especially in complex and underserved clinical areas.

Research Areas

1. Health Economics and Health Technology Assessment

Exploring the economic implications of healthcare interventions, resource allocation, treatment access, and value-based healthcare, with particular interest in rare and complex diseases.

2. Secondary Immunodeficiency and Immunology

Investigating acquired immune dysfunction, secondary immunodeficiency, immune risk stratification, diagnostic challenges, and functional immune deficits.

3. Plasma-Derived Therapies and Rare Diseases

Examining plasma-derived medicinal products in rare and immune-related conditions, including treatment access, evidence generation, and healthcare system implications.

4. Real-World Evidence and Health Data Analytics

Exploring the use of real-world data to generate actionable evidence for healthcare research, clinical decision-making, and health policy.

5. Artificial Intelligence and Digital Health

Investigating machine learning, deep learning, advanced analytics, and AI-enabled approaches for healthcare research, evidence synthesis, risk assessment, and digital transformation.

Research Approach

  • Systematic literature reviews and evidence synthesis.
  • Health economics and healthcare evaluation.
  • Real-world evidence generation and analysis.
  • Advanced statistical analysis and predictive modelling.
  • Machine learning and artificial intelligence applications.
  • Data-driven approaches to healthcare decision-making.

Current Research Direction

A central direction of my research is to explore how multidisciplinary evidence and advanced data analytics can improve the understanding of secondary immunodeficiency, rare diseases, and plasma-derived therapies.

I am particularly interested in bridging clinical evidence, health economics, and digital transformation to support more informed, equitable, and evidence-based healthcare systems.

Research Vision

My broader research vision is to contribute to the responsible integration of health data analytics and artificial intelligence into healthcare research, supporting better evidence generation, improved resource allocation, and more informed policy and clinical decisions.
