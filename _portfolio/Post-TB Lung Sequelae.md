## Project Overview

An ongoing international research collaboration with the University of New South Wales (UNSW), Australia, investigating post-tuberculosis lung sequelae through a scoping review.

## Research Focus

- Mapping existing evidence on long-term pulmonary consequences following tuberculosis.
- Exploring respiratory complications and persistent lung impairment.
- Identifying evidence gaps and emerging research priorities.

## Methodology

Scoping review methodology.

## Collaboration

University of New South Wales (UNSW), Australia.
