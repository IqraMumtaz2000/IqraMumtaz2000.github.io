## Project Overview

An ongoing collaborative research project with University Medical Center Hamburg-Eppendorf (UKE), Hamburg, Germany, exploring the application of machine learning in infectious disease research.

## Research Focus

- Exploring machine learning approaches for infectious disease research.
- Investigating data-driven patterns in infectious disease datasets.
- Evaluating the potential of predictive analytics to support healthcare research.
- Exploring opportunities for evidence-based disease analysis and decision-making.

## Methodology

Machine learning and advanced data analytics.

## Collaboration

University Medical Center Hamburg-Eppendorf (UKE), Hamburg, Germany.
