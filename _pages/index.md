# Iqra Mumtaz

**Doctoral Researcher in Management of Digital Transformation (MDT)**  
**IMT School for Advanced Studies Lucca, Italy**

My research interests lie at the intersection of health economics, immunology, evidence synthesis, real-world evidence, and healthcare data science. I focus particularly on secondary immunodeficiency, immunoglobulin replacement therapy, immune dysfunction, and the application of advanced analytical methods to healthcare research.

My work combines multidisciplinary research, systematic evidence synthesis, statistical analysis, machine learning, and scientific communication to translate complex biomedical and healthcare evidence into meaningful insights for research, policy, and decision-making.

## About Me

I am a multidisciplinary researcher and data scientist with over 10 years of experience across evidence synthesis, health research, scientific consulting, and data analytics.

Currently, I am pursuing doctoral research in Management of Digital Transformation (MDT) at IMT School for Advanced Studies Lucca, Italy.

My academic and professional experience spans immunology research, health economics, systematic reviews, meta-analysis, statistical modelling, machine learning, predictive analytics, and scientific communication.

My research particularly examines secondary immunodeficiency (SID), hypogammaglobulinemia, immunoglobulin replacement therapy (IgRT), immunosenescence, and immune dysfunction across different clinical conditions.

I am also interested in integrating real-world evidence, machine learning, and advanced data analytics into healthcare research, particularly to improve evidence generation, risk assessment, and the interpretation of complex health data.

Previously, I have worked as a Research Scientist at Osmo Technologies, Research Assistant at Gulf Consultancy, and Copywriter and Research Consultant at MBG. My professional responsibilities have included systematic reviews, scientific manuscript development, quantitative research, healthcare analytics, and technical reporting.

## Research and Publications

My research focuses on synthesizing multidisciplinary evidence to understand immune dysfunction, secondary immunodeficiency, therapeutic risk, and healthcare research priorities.

### Peer-reviewed Research Publications

**1. Rethinking Secondary Immunodeficiency: A Cross-Domain Pathway Framework for Risk Stratification**

*Frontiers in Immunology* (2026)

This systematic review develops a cross-domain pathway framework for understanding secondary immunodeficiency across different clinical conditions. It examines convergent humoral, cellular, and innate immune dysfunction and proposes an exploratory framework for organizing evidence relevant to risk stratification.

- [Read the published article](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/immunology/articles/10.3389/fimmu.2026.1866662/full)
- [DOI: 10.3389/fimmu.2026.1866662](https://doi.org/10.3389/fimmu.2026.1866662)

**2. Redefining Secondary Immunodeficiency: A Systematic Review of Therapy-Driven Risk, Functional Immune Deficits, Diagnostic Inconsistencies, and Global Gaps in Standardized Care**

*Journal of Clinical Immunology* (2026)

This systematic review examines therapy-driven secondary immunodeficiency, functional immune deficits, diagnostic inconsistencies, and gaps in standardized approaches to care.

- [Read the published article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10875-026-02071-5)
- [DOI: 10.1007/s10875-026-02071-5](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10875-026-02071-5)
