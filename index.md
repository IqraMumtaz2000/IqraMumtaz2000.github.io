# Iqra Mumtaz

**Doctoral Researcher in Management of Digital Transformation (MDT)**  
**IMT School for Advanced Studies Lucca, Italy**

I am a multidisciplinary researcher working at the intersection of health economics, immunology, real-world evidence (RWE), and healthcare data science. My research focuses on generating and interpreting evidence related to immune dysfunction, secondary immunodeficiency, and healthcare outcomes.

My work combines systematic evidence synthesis, quantitative research, statistical modelling, and machine learning to translate complex biomedical and healthcare data into meaningful insights. I am particularly interested in applying advanced analytical methods to support evidence-based research, healthcare policy, and decision-making.

---

## About Me

With over 10 years of experience across research, evidence synthesis, scientific consulting, and data analytics, I bring an interdisciplinary perspective to healthcare research. My academic and professional work integrates immunology, health economics, research methodology, and emerging data-driven approaches to address complex healthcare questions.

My research expertise includes health economics, secondary immunodeficiency, immunology, plasma-derived therapies, real-world evidence, systematic reviews and meta-analysis, machine learning, and healthcare data analytics.
